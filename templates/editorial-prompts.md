# Bounded prompts for a writing helper

Copy only the needed prompt and approved source material into your chosen tool. These prompts do not require a particular provider. Keep personal data and private paths out of cloud inputs unless authorized. Replace placeholders before use.

## Extract facts

> Read only the attached source extracts for [story]. Produce a table of claim, fact/observation/judgment/hypothesis, exact source reference and date/version, safe public wording and unresolved questions. Return candidate, no_candidate or blocker for this source scope. Do not browse unrelated files, invent tests or infer that unknown means failed. Do not write the story yet.

## Establish the author angle

> The reader is [reader] and their question is [question]. My own words are [author statement]. The verified result is [result]. Help me express one central story while preserving my point of view. Give a short proposed angle and identify what evidence the opening must show. Do not invent feelings, motives, reactions, dialogue, time spent or failure counts. Keep unresolved claims in editor notes.

## Unify title, cover and narration

> Using the accepted angle [angle] and claim table [table], return one title, short cover text, spoken opening, complete spoken draft and editor-only notes. They must tell the same story. Preserve [phrases]. Do not add [forbidden inferences]. Use everyday language and remove generic hype or unrelated engagement prompts. A duration is an estimate until recorded. Mark any wording that depends on footage still to be captured. Return text only; do not generate media or operate accounts.

## Review instead of endlessly rewriting

> Compare this exact draft revision [revision] with the supplied evidence and author statement. Identify unsupported experiences, claims stronger than evidence, stale version claims, mixed topics, mismatches among title/cover/opening, and language the author would not say. Quote the affected sentence and suggest the smallest correction. Separate factual findings from taste suggestions. Do not claim unseen footage exists or change an approved claim without flagging it.

## Produce a short filming list

> Use the selected script [revision]. List only missing shots needed to show its claims: action, framing, result to capture and privacy precautions. Separate required proof from optional decoration. Preserve existing usable footage. Do not ask for an installation reenactment, unnecessary reset, or another person's appearance unless the story genuinely requires it and it is authorized. Put the list next to the script and state who supplies it.

## Resume after a tool interruption

> Current selected revision: [reference]. Last confirmed completed step: [step and evidence]. Saved platform state: [state or unknown]. Pending action: [action]. Read the bounded source list, inspect saved state before retrying any external action, and continue only within the existing authorization. Report actual results, uncertain states and the next owner. Do not create a duplicate project to work around an unverified previous submission.
