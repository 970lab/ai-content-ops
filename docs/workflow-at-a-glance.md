# The workflow in ten simple steps

The author supplies real experience and direction. AI helps organize, draft, check and prepare; the author accepts the final content and decides where it may be published. Start with one text post. Media and platform operations depend on the tools you bring.

## 1. Choose one useful story

Start from something you did, learned or tried. Name one question the reader can answer after reading.

## 2. Supply the real sources

Provide only the notes and media needed. Separate confirmed details from unknowns; a short source is enough to begin.

## 3. Agree on the angle

The author decides what matters and what they want to express. AI helps organize that point of view.

## 4. Write one complete piece

For text: title, opening, body and ending. Add cover text or narration only for the chosen format. Keep the promises consistent.

## 5. Check facts and voice

Remove unsupported experiences, feelings, figures and test results. Read it in the author’s voice and revise the selected draft.

## 6. Complete the needed media

Skip this for plain text. Otherwise use a short missing-shots list, then check actual images, edits, captions and audio. Label renders and historical assets.

## 7. Save a resumable package

Keep the current draft, sources and required attachments together. Record revision, completed work and the next owner/action; transfer the whole folder when changing computers.

## 8. Prepare the destination and get acceptance

Check current platform requirements and show the complete final candidate to the author. Draft, upload, scheduling and publication have distinct scopes.

## 9. Publish only as authorized, then reopen it

Use an available permitted tool or the official platform workflow. Verify the actual item and distinguish draft, scheduled, pending review and public states.

## 10. Archive and learn

Save final text, assets and actual release evidence. Read back any archive transfer and use observed metrics and feedback, leaving missing data unknown.

For the first local draft, follow [Start with one post](quickstart.md). For detailed responsibilities and structured handoffs, see [the complete guide](creator-workflow.md). These ten actions are a readable view of the existing five modules, not new artifact types or an automatic publishing service.
